# Product


## Platform
web

## Users
Players exploring enemies that learn and develop counter-tactics, with optional learning inspection and experimental comparisons. The creator is prototyping the mechanic before wider use.

## Product Purpose
A playable tactical arena that makes remembered behavior matter across missions. Success means players notice, understand, and counter enemy adaptation. Establish measurable effectiveness through repeatable comparisons, then publish a microsite with a playable demonstration, transparent evidence and downloadable reusable library releases.

## Capabilities and Constraints
A separate Rust library selects among authored legal tactics and learns from observed outcomes. Browser gameplay uses the real Wasm library. Combat statistics remain fixed. Scout delivery controls knowledge transfer. Missions should carry memory forward. Unity and Godot integrations are explicitly deferred.

## Brand Commitments
Tactical Air. Game first, little introductory prose, controls near the top, useful data and graphs available. The user rejected the pale generic dashboard and chose an illustrated tactical map with terrain and units carrying a hand-drawn military feel. Demo movement names must be understandable.

## Evidence on Hand
Actual combat recaps, automated replay and learning tests, and scripted evaluations in docs/. No human enjoyment or fairness results have been collected.

## Product Principles
- Learning follows observed combat, not a scripted difficulty ladder.
- Counterplay and blocking communication can undermine enemy learning.
- Keep experimentation visible without overwhelming play.
- Reuse the library without coupling it to this game's presentation.
- Preserve unfavorable experimental results; distinguish measured effectiveness from player enjoyment.
- Tie public claims and release downloads to reproducible, versioned evidence.

Study boundaries and controls

All arms retain ordinary Rust learning. Both reviewing arms omit policy cells, scores, counts and probabilities. Full review receives completed warm-up and own-branch fights plus its previous bounded note. Recent-only receives the shared initial warm-up request; later requests receive only the latest own fight, current plan and true round clock. Recent output notes are recorded but never fed back.

Plans can change engagement distance, aim/lead selection and lead prediction. Health, damage, speed, cooldowns and visibility are fixed. A held plan can still produce different learned tactics. New keyed schedules are not unseen-world validation, and model repetitions are nested trajectories. No p-values or confidence intervals are claimed.

Design and verification

Each enemy has two legal tactics. The task qualifies before any learner runs: the best tactic must change between A and B, with observable rewards and meaningful damage differences. The stable player uses A 90% of the time and B 10%; occasional B fights are not a change in that distribution. Gradual drift changes that mixture over 32 fights. Recurrence switches back after 32.

All branches share their initial memory and random state. Full automatic reset clears both tactic cells in the detected role/context. Selective forgetting clears just the action that triggered. Signaled reset uses the hidden distribution and offline action tables to reset before its best response changes; it is a privileged reference, not a deployable detector or guaranteed ceiling.

Detection windows begin at fight 1, and also 33 for recurrence. A first alarm inside a window counts as operational detection, even though random variation may have caused it. All misses and all later alarms remain in the data. Stable-history alarms are false relative to the stationary distribution.

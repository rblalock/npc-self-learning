Experience and allocation cost

Every branch starts from the same preparation checkpoint in its case: ten baseline warmups followed by a balanced survey of all seven plans. The survey consumes real fights and updates the Rust learner. Agent and algorithm receive those completed observations, then only their own subsequent history. The shared preparation is counted once per case in the physical run budget.

Prior calibrated fixed reference

The far plan was frozen using the earlier v2 calibration, before v2 evaluation. It is reused unchanged in v4. Its 1,384 prior calibration fights are outside this run and outside the matched-experience comparison.

Study boundaries and controls

These are familiar development worlds and reused encounter schedules, with two histories and two nested model repetitions. They are not unfamiliar validation worlds. Every controller retains Rust learning. Agent and algorithm observations include the full chronological preparation and own history, the actual current plan, complete plan menu, true physical round and the private previous note. Hidden labels, seeds, future schedules, internal policy scores and other branches are not planner evidence. Model reasons, predictions and notes are claims, not measured findings. Plans are authored choices; this study does not establish invented tactics, model weight learning, human enjoyment or arbitrary-game transfer.

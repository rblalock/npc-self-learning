Study boundaries and controls

All arms retain ordinary Rust learning. Immediate and gated review both receive completed warm-up and own-branch fights, the actual current plan and their previous bounded note. Neither receives gate counters, arm identity, policy cells, scores, probabilities, hidden schedule labels or other branches. Their initial request is shared with held-initial. Notes are model claims, and a blocked recommendation never rewrites the actual action history. The three-observation minimum is an execution rule, not a change detector or statistical evidence threshold.

Plans can change engagement distance, aim/lead selection and lead prediction. Health, damage, speed, cooldowns and visibility are fixed. A held plan can still produce different learned tactics. These familiar worlds and their exact v2 schedules are reused development evidence; model repetitions are nested trajectories, not independent worlds. No p-values or confidence intervals are claimed.
